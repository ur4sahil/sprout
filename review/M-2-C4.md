### Lesson

- see: A bar graph shows how many with bars. A longer bar means more. (picture: bar graph: apples 7, cookies 4, balls 9)
- watch: How many chose apples? Find apples. Read the end of its bar: 7. (picture: bar graph: apples 7, cookies 4, balls 9)
- try: How many chose balls? (picture: bar graph: apples 7, cookies 4, balls 9)
  - then: Find balls. Read the end of its bar: 9.
- remember: Read one row at a time. (picture: bar graph: apples 7, cookies 4, balls 9)
