# Wave H3: Language Arts pre-K and kindergarten, 30 skills

Plan section 15.2, wave H3. Pre-K grows from 10 to 22 skills, kindergarten from 18 to 36. Every answer is checked by the
new `earlyWords` oracle (`tests/oracles/waves/h3-earlyWords.ts`), except K A.3, which uses `matchEqual`. Lists are in
`src/content/data/h3-words.ts`, `h3-stories.ts` and `h3-sounds.ts`; generators in `src/content/generators/ela/pk/h3-*.ts`
and `ela/k/h3-*.ts`. Pre-K and kindergarten questions never have more than 3 options, every picture a child must name says
its word, a listen question never shows its word and its pictures never speak, and a read question never speaks what is read.

## Ladders

Pre-K skills sit before every kindergarten skill of their ladder. letters: PK A.6, A.7 after A.5; K A.3 to A.5 after A.2.
sounds: PK B.4, B.6 after B.1, B.5 after B.2; K B.13 after B.2, B.8 to B.10 after B.3, B.12 after B.6. phonics: K B.11
after B.5. sight-words: K C.4 after C.3. grammar: K D.4, D.6 after D.1, D.5 after D.3. vocabulary: PK C.3 to C.7, D.1,
D.2 after C.2, then K D.7. reading: K E.6, E.7 after E.1, E.4, E.5 after E.3. The reading ladder starts in kindergarten,
so the pre-K listening skills sit on vocabulary.

## Choices made where nothing was given

- Pairs that are already Reasoning picture analogies (bee and honey, key and lock) are left out of "Things that go together".
- The letter picture is called "envelope", so "letter" always means a letter of the alphabet in Language Arts.
- Blending three sounds into a word is left out: a voice cannot say a lone sound (plan section 9). Counting sounds shows the
  split on screen and says each sound.
- Kindergarten sight words set 4 is left out: grade 1 B.1 already is that skill.
- Position words are left out: Math K E.4 already teaches them.
