# Wave H2: Math Pre-K and Grade 2, 35 skills

Plan section 15.2, wave H2. Pre-K grows from 16 to 32 skills and Grade 2 from 27 to 46. Each skill has three levels, a
four-beat lesson built from two of its own questions, and version 1. Pre-K questions are picture-first with spoken prompts
and at most 3 options. Every picture is one that already has a drawn picture in `public/art`, so `npm run art` was not
needed.

Files: generators in `src/content/generators/math/pk/h2-*.ts` and `src/content/generators/math/g2/h2-*.ts`
(`h2-lesson.ts` holds the shared skill and lesson builder), lists in `src/content/data/h2-world.ts` and
`src/content/data/h2-stories.ts`, oracles in `tests/oracles/waves/h2.ts`, level rules in `tests/content/h2.test.ts`,
browser sweep in `e2e/h2.spec.ts`.

## Oracles

Reused: `add`, `subtract`, `count`, `sequence`, `compare`, `attribute`, `pattern`, `numberName`, `place`, `missing`, `graph`.
New, each named with `h2` first so no other wave can take the name (`src/engine/oracleFacts/h2.ts`, `tests/oracles/waves/h2.ts`),
each with its own copy of the facts it checks:

| Oracle | Facts | Right answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `h2World` | ask (shape, colour, solid, heavy, kind), thing or want, options; items and binAttrs for kind | A thing's shape, colour or solid; the option that has it; the heaviest or lightest; a bin for each thing |
| `h2Ordinal` | line, ask | The first, second, third or last in the line |
| `h2SamePattern` | target, rows | The row that repeats the same way as the target |
| `h2OrderBy` | values, dir | The items smallest first (up) or largest first (down) |
| `h2Regroup` | hundreds, tens, ones (may be over 9), ask | The number, or how many tens it has |
| `h2Even` | ask (double, half, isDouble, sumParity, pickSum) | The double, the half, yes or no, even or odd, the sum with that parity |
| `h2Digits` | digits, ask | The greatest or least number from the digits, never starting with 0 |
| `h2Chart` | topLeft, blank | topLeft plus 10 a row down plus 1 a column right |
| `h2AmPm` | ask (half, time, which) | a.m. or p.m., the time with it, the thing in that half of the day |
| `h2Coins` | target, sets | The set of coins worth the target |
| `h2Metric` | ask (unit, estimate, convert) | Centimeters or meters, about how long, meters in centimeters |
| `h2Solid` | solid, ask (faces, corners, edges, which) | The count, or the solid with that many flat faces. Refuses a cone's point and a cylinder's rims |
| `h2Split` | a, b, options | The written split that equals a + b |

## Choices made where the brief was open

- Left out as near-duplicates: elapsed time in whole hours (Grade 3 F.2 level 1 is exactly that), AAB and ABB patterns
  (Kindergarten G.1), compare with symbols (Grade 2 A.3 level 3), equal shares (Grade 2 D.2), ten frames (Kindergarten A.2),
  arrays and equal groups (Grade 2 B.8). Number names to 1,000 is kept although Grade 3 A.2 does the same to 10,000:
  2.NBT.A.3 is a Grade 2 standard.
- Pre-K C.5 never puts a square and a rectangle in one question, because a square is a rectangle too.
- Pre-K C.7 uses only colour sets with no hard pair (no red with green or orange, no orange with yellow).
- Grade 2 D.4 asks only what every school agrees on: a cube's 6 faces, 12 edges and 8 corners, faces of the four solids,
  and no corners on a sphere or cylinder. A cone's point and a cylinder's rims are never asked.
- Grade 2 A.7's words are read, not heard: the numeral and the word options have no spoken label.
- Ladders: each skill goes right after the skill it builds on in its own grade (`src/content/waves/h2/ladders.ts`).
