# Wave H4: Language Arts grades 1 and 2 (30 skills)

Plan section 15.2, wave H4. Built Nov 17, 2026. Each skill continues its grade's lettered category
(A. Phonics, B. Words, C. Grammar, D. Reading). Every question is checked by the `h4ela` oracle
(`tests/oracles/waves/h4ela.ts`), which works out the answer by rule where one exists (endings, ABC order,
guide words, the sound of y and of c and g, capitals, one or more, who tells a story, describing word or
adverb) and otherwise looks it up in the wave's lists (`src/content/data/h4-*.ts`).

Generators: `src/content/generators/ela/g1/h4-phonics.ts`, `h4-words.ts`, `h4-grammar.ts`, `h4-reading.ts`
(and `h4-parts.ts`, what they share), and the same four in `g2/`.

Gaps checked against the grades' existing skills: irregular plurals, past tense, contractions, prefixes,
suffixes, homophones and apostrophes for owning already exist in grade 2, so none of them is repeated.
Bossy r is grade 2 A.2, so grade 1 does not get it. Joining words (and, but, or) are grade 1 only.
