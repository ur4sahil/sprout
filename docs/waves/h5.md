# Wave H5: Math grade 3, 15 skills

Plan section 15.2, wave H5. Grade 3 Math goes from 32 to 47 skills. Each skill fills a gap that Common Core or IXL's grade 3 list
teaches and the grade did not have yet. None repeats an existing skill: C.2 level 3 already asks "3 × 4 is the same as 4 × 3",
so C.8 covers times 1, times 0 and grouping instead; F.2 asks elapsed time from a clock, so F.10 reads a schedule; B.2 never
subtracts across a zero, so B.5 does nothing else.

## Where they sit

- Ladders: B.5 after B.2 and B.6 after B.4 (add-subtract); C.8, C.9, C.11, C.10, D.4, D.5, C.12 after C.7 (multiply-divide);
  E.5 to E.7 after E.4 (fractions); F.8, F.9 after F.3 (length); F.10 after F.2 (time). Every anchor is a grade 3 skill, so a lower
  grade skill another wave adds can never land after one of these.
- Generators: `src/content/generators/math/g3/h5-addSubtract.ts` (B.5, B.6), `h5-multiply.ts` (C.8 to C.12, D.4, D.5),
  `h5-fractions.ts` (E.5 to E.7), `h5-measure.ts` (F.8 to F.10), with `h5-parts.ts` for what they share (`fill` for story
  templates, `sentenceSay` and `sentencePicture` for written number sentences). Stories are in `src/content/data/h5-stories.ts`.

## Oracles

- `expression3` (new): works out a written number sentence exactly, with brackets, × before +, and a fraction as a division
  ("(5 × 4) + (2 × 4)", "2/3 × 12", "7 × 8 = 35 + □"). Asks: `value`, `equal` (the option worth the same as `expr`), `missing`
  (the one number from 0 to 1,000 for □; a sentence with none or several is refused), `parity`, `pickParity`, `most` and `least`
  (of `exprs`), `roundTimes` (a × b rounded to the nearest ten). Used by C.8, C.9, C.11, C.12, D.5, E.5 to E.7, F.8, F.9.
- `operation3` (new): the sign a kind of story needs (join +, separate and compare −, groups and array ×, share and groupsOf ÷),
  and the number sentence "a sign b". Used by B.6.
- Reused: `subtract` (B.5), `multiply` (C.10 total, D.4 quotient), `elapsed` (F.10).

## Choices made where nothing was given

- Spoken brackets: "(5 × 4) + (2 × 4)" is said "five times four, plus two times four"; "2 × (3 × 4)" is said "two times the answer
  to three times four". On screen a bracket is written against its number, so the sentence does not spread out.
- C.8 level 1 asks times 0 a third of the time: the answer 0 always sits first, so more would break the spread of answer places.
- A money word problem skill was left out: the money ladder ends at a grade 2 skill, and wave H2 may add grade 2 money skills
  after it, which would put a grade 3 skill before a grade 2 one. A pictograph with half pictures was left out: the graph picture
  draws whole pictures only, and pictures may not be changed in a wave.
