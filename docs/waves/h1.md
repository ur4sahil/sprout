# Wave H1: Math kindergarten and grade 1, 40 skills

Plan section 15.2, wave H1. Twenty kindergarten skills and twenty grade 1 skills that fill gaps the grades did not
cover yet, in IXL's spirit of one idea shown several ways. Each has three levels. Kindergarten keeps to three options.

Generators: `src/content/generators/math/k/h1-*.ts` and `src/content/generators/math/g1/h1-*.ts` (shared pieces in
`k/h1-parts.ts`). Lists: `src/content/data/h1-numberWords.ts`, `h1-things.ts`, `h1-stories.ts`. New oracles (all named
`h1...` so no other wave's oracle can clash): `tests/oracles/waves/h1.ts`, facts in `src/engine/oracleFacts/h1.ts`.

## Ladders

Each skill goes after the skill it builds on, never down a grade: kindergarten counting A.8 to A.13 after K A.7;
B.4, B.5 after K B.3; C.12 to C.17 after K C.11; E.6, E.7 after K E.5; F.4, F.5 on length after K F.3; F.6 on data
after K F.2; G.2 after K G.1. Grade 1: A.6 and A.10 on counting after 1 A.2; A.7 on place value after 1 A.3, A.9 after
1 A.5; A.8 on comparing after 1 A.4; B.14 to B.19 after 1 B.13; C.6 to C.8 on time after 1 C.1; C.9, C.10 on data
after 1 C.3; C.11 on money after 1 C.4; C.12 on length after 1 C.5; D.4 on shapes after 1 D.3; D.5 on fractions after
1 D.1.

## Choices where the plan was silent

- Kindergarten says "take away" for the minus sign, as the existing kindergarten skills do.
- C.15 and C.16 never offer the turned-around sum (b + a), which also describes the picture.
- Heavier and holds-more things come in size tiers; a question only compares things from different tiers.
- A.10 and grade 1 A.10 are read skills: the word, the numerals and the word options are all silent.
- B.16 level 1 uses only "some more came", level 2 only "some went away"; the start-unknown stories are level 3 only.
